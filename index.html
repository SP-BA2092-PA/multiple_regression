Core Analytics Concepts

Regression Equation & Parameters

For SLR: $\hat{y} = \beta_0 + \beta_1 X_{wt}$. For MLR: $\hat{y} = \beta_0 + \beta_1 X_{wt} + \beta_2 X_{qsec}$. $\beta_0$ represents baseline value; $\beta_i$ represents slope adjusted for other features.

$R^2$ (Coefficient of Determination) & RMSE

$R^2 = 1 - (SS_{res}/SS_{tot})$. Measures proportion of variance explained by model. $RMSE = \sqrt{\frac{1}{n}\sum(y_i-\hat{y}_i)^2}$ measures average prediction error magnitude in original units ($mpg$).

Correlation ($r$)

Quantifies degree of linear association between two variables, ranging from -1 to +1. High correlation between independent variables indicates potential multicollinearity.

Multicollinearity & VIF

Occurs when independent variables ($wt$ and $qsec$) are strongly correlated. $VIF = \frac{1}{1-R_j^2}$. VIF > 5-10 indicates high multicollinearity, inflating coefficient variance.
